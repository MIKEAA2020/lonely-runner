\section{The main theorem, stated conditionally}\label{sec:SC}

The closures of \S\ref{sec:rigid}--\S\ref{sec:higherrung} settle the
covering obstruction at the base rung, at the composite micro-cases, and
at the critical cells of the higher rungs. What remains is the frontier:
the $(1K,5U)$ cell at $T=8$ and its generalizations $(1K,T{-}3U)$ at
every $T\ge 8$, the cells whose five-and-more-difference censuses are too
large to enumerate and whose closure must therefore be structural. This
section states the program's central result in its stated form:
a single named conditional theorem, preceded by a ledger of
hypotheses in which every input carries its status---proved, measured,
or derived from a disclosed fitted constant. The theorem is the
consolidation of the conditional statements scattered through the
cascade analysis of \S\ref{sec:scale}; stating it once, with named
hypotheses, is the point of this section.

The statement needs the higher-rung vocabulary of
\S\ref{sec:higherrung}, which follows later; we give the gloss here and
defer all derivations. At $N=p^2$ with $p=Tk+\epsilon$ prime, a covering
family consists of \emph{kernel} members (speeds divisible by $p$) and
\emph{unit} members (speeds coprime to $p$); a cell $(aK,bU)$ collects
the families with $a$ kernel and $b$ unit members. The kernel covers the
fibers of a kernel bad set and leaves $|U|=(T-2)k+\epsilon-1$ uncovered
fibers $F_j\cong\Z_p$; on each, the $b$ unit footprints are arithmetic
progressions which must cover $\Z_p$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:rungT}). The
\emph{census normal form} pins the normalizer arc's difference to $1$
and takes canonical differences in $[2,D]$, $D=(p-1)/2$; for the
frontier cell, $m=T-3$ arcs carry a two-value size window
$\{2k,2k+1\}$ or $\{2k+1,2k+2\}$ and an \emph{overlap budget}
$\mathrm{ov}=\Sigma s-p\approx(T-6)k+O(1)$. A family is
\emph{census-admissible} if it admits at least one covering placement in
the normal form; the \emph{fiber cascade} is the iterated filter of
\S\ref{sec:scale-model}, which retains the anchor placements surviving
the uncovered fibers one by one and kills the family when the survivor
set empties.

\begin{theorem}[scaling closure; conditional, $\pm$-reduced]\label{thm:SC}
Assume the hypotheses $\mathrm{NF}$, $\mathrm{Hq}_\pm$, $\mathrm{H1g}$,
$\mathrm{H2r}$ of Table~\ref{tab:ledger} (statuses as in the table;
$\mathrm{Hq}_\pm$ is the $\pm$-representative, additive-error form and
subsumes $\mathrm{H2m}$ as its one-step case), together with the
committed identity $\mathrm{EQ}$ of the same table. Let $T\ge 8$, let
$p=Tk+\epsilon$ be prime with $k\ge 2$ and $0\le\epsilon<T$ (so $k=\floor{p/T}$
is canonical; $p\ge p_0(T)$, the least such prime, $p_0(8)=17$), and let the
frontier cell $(1K,T{-}3U)$ at $N=p^2$ be given, with $m=T-3$ unit arcs, the
two-value size window of Lemma~\ref{lem:rungT}, and overlap budget
$\mathrm{ov}=\Sigma s-p=(T-6)k+O(1)$. Let $U_\pm$ be a $\pm$-representative
non-resonant fiber set (one representative per pair $\{j,-j\}$, the
reflection fiber excluded), $|U_\pm|=\tfrac12\bigl((T-2)k+\epsilon-1\bigr)$.
Then:
\smallskip
\emph{(i)} every census-admissible family in the cell dies in the fiber
cascade, processed over $U_\pm$, within
\[
C(T,k)\;=\;
\frac{\log\!\bigl(c_1\,\mathrm{ov}^{\,m-1}\bigr)}
     {(m-1)\,\log\!\bigl(p/\mathrm{ov}\bigr)-\log(c_1\Gamma)}
\;+\;O(1)
\]
fibers, where $\Gamma$ is the uniform constant of $\mathrm{Hq}_\pm$ and the
$O(1)$ absorbs the additive-error floor $E/(1-r)$ of
Proposition~\ref{prop:Hq} plus the deterministic kill of the $O(1)$
remainder. The denominator is uniformly positive under $\mathrm{H1g}$'s
margin clause (which includes $\Gamma$). The depth is $O_T(\log k)$ with the
explicit leading term $\log k/\log(T/(T-6))$; the $k\to\infty$ form
$(m-1)\log(T/(T-6))-\log(c_1\Gamma)$ gives the $T$-flat plateau
$\approx 9,9.2,10.1$ at $T=8,9,10$ (at $k=2,3,3$). \emph{No $k$-uniform
linear bound is claimed}; the earlier ``$\le T+3$'' is withdrawn (it compared
the depth against the un-reduced budget $|U|$).
\smallskip
\emph{(ii)} since $C(T,k)\ll|U_\pm|=\tfrac12(T-2)k$ for every $T\ge8$,
$k\ge2$, with a surplus growing linearly in $k$ (conditional on the ledger:
the depth bound is what (i) provides; the surplus language refers to the
$\pm$-reduced fiber budget), the cascade exhausts the budget: no
census-admissible family admits a covering placement valid on all of
$U_\pm$ (equivalently on all of $U$, by the reflection identification).
\smallskip
\emph{(iii)} the cell $(1K,T{-}3U)$ at $p^2$ is empty. \emph{This is a
statement about the covering obstruction at the cell only.} It does not by
itself imply $\mathrm{LRC}$ at the rung: $(\mathrm{TAU}$-$n)$ requires a
certifying pair for every $V$ at its own pair-sum moduli, and ruling out one
cell type at moduli $p^2$ leaves prime moduli (the rigid zone), $pq$ and
other composites, and the remaining cells untouched. The cell-emptiness
result is exactly the covering obstruction at the frontier cell.
The measured range of the calibration is $T=8,9,10$ at $p\le 73$, with
committed ground-truth cells at $p\le 61$ for $T\in\{7,8\}$; the statement
at primes beyond the calibration is exactly what the hypotheses purchase.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[the Hq$_\pm$-induction; the derivation step]\label{prop:Hq}
Let the frontier cell and notation be as in Theorem~\ref{thm:SC}, and assume
$\mathrm{Hq}_\pm$ with constant $\Gamma$ and additive error term $E$, and the
volume law $\mathrm{H1g}$ with
$|\mathrm{Sol}(f,\mathrm{sz})|\le c_1\,\mathrm{ov}^{\,m-1}$ for every
admissible family and size tuple. Let the cascade process the
$\pm$-representative non-resonant fibers of $U_\pm$ (one per reflection pair,
reflection fiber excluded). Write $S_i$ for the survivor set after $i$
processed fibers, $A_i=\lambda_i^{-1}(\mathrm{Sol}_i-\tau_i)$ for the $i$-th
fiber's dilate constraint, $d=m-1$, $B=c_1\,\mathrm{ov}^{\,d}$, and
$r=\Gamma B/p^{\,d}$. Then $|S_0|\le B$, each step obeys
$|S_i|\le r\,|S_{i-1}|+E$, and consequently
\[
|S_q|\;\le\;B\,r^{\,q}+\frac{E}{1-r}\;\xrightarrow[q\to\infty]{}\;\frac{E}{1-r}
\;=:\;S_\infty=O(1).
\]
Once $|S_q|\le S_\infty$ the set is a structured $O(1)$ remainder; a single
further fiber (a generic non-resonant $\lambda$) moves the fixed drift off
the finite survivor set, dropping it to $\varnothing$ (the deterministic
kill). Hence the cascade is empty whenever
\[
q\;>\;\frac{\log B}{(m-1)\,\log(p/\mathrm{ov})-\log(c_1\Gamma)}\;+\;O(1),
\]
the $O(1)$ absorbing the error floor and the deterministic kill. The
denominator is uniformly positive iff
$c_1\Gamma<\bigl(p/\mathrm{ov}\bigr)^{m-1}$ with a margin uniform in
$(T,k)$; in the $k\to\infty$ limit $p/\mathrm{ov}\to T/(T-6)$, and the
uniform condition is the threshold clause of $\mathrm{H1g}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
$S_0$ is the family's initial placement set, bounded by the volume law.
For each $i$, $S_i=S_{i-1}\cap A_i$ with $A_i$ the $i$-th fiber's
constraint set, so $\mathrm{Hq}_\pm$ applied to the admissible history
$S_{i-1}$ (a $\pm$-representative fiber, so $A_i$ is a genuine constraint,
not a reflection duplicate) gives
$|S_i|\le\Gamma\,|S_{i-1}|\,|\mathrm{Sol}_i|/p^{d}+E\le r\,|S_{i-1}|+E$
using $|\mathrm{Sol}_i|\le B$. Induction gives
$|S_q|\le B\,r^{q}+E(1+r+\dots+r^{q-1})\le B\,r^{q}+E/(1-r)$ (for $r<1$,
which is the positivity clause). The contraction continues until the
$r^{q}$ term falls below $S_\infty=E/(1-r)$, i.e.\ after
$q=\log(B(1-r)/E)/\log(1/r)$ steps; the residual $O(1)$ set is then killed
by one further fiber's drift, which has no fixed point on a nonempty finite
set unless the set is a union of drift-fixed configurations---excluded for a
generic non-resonant $\lambda$. The resonance tail (weak-cut fibers with
$\bar\lambda\le\Lambda_0$) is excluded from $U_\pm$ by construction, so no
$N_{\mathrm{res}}$ term is needed.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The reduction and its hypotheses.}
An unquantified ``sequential composition'' input
(the $\mathrm{H0}$ row: $C_i\approx
\bar\gamma^{\,i}|\mathrm{Sol}|^{i+1}/p^{(m-1)i}$) is not used; a recurrence for the
\emph{independent-extension model} and a bound on \emph{unconditional} pair
intersections do not compose into a bound on the \emph{iterated,
history-dependent} intersections the actual cascade performs: pairwise
sparsity does not control higher-order correlations, and three sets can have
acceptable pairwise overlaps while sharing a structured common core. The
multi-fiber survivor inequality $\mathrm{Hq}_\pm$ (above) replaces both, with
$\mathrm{H2m}$ as its one-step case. The derivation from the ledger is
exactly Proposition~\ref{prop:Hq}'s induction, conditional on named open
inputs only.

The cascade is modelled on the $\pm$-representative fiber set $U_\pm$
(one representative per reflection pair; the reflection fiber excluded, by
Lemma~\ref{lem:reflect}), because the fiber $-j$ imposes the same covering
constraint as fiber $j$; an oriented-fiber model would double-count every
constraint and make the survivor inequality fail on the reflection duplicate.
The survivor inequality $\mathrm{Hq}_\pm$ carries an additive error term $E$;
the induction contracts to an $O(1)$ floor and closes it by a deterministic
kill (Prop.~\ref{prop:Hq}), and the depth is $O_T(\log k)$
(Prop.~\ref{prop:depth}). Clause (iii) states cell emptiness and nothing more.

\paragraph{What is conditional and what is not.}
The reduction chain \emph{(i)}$\to$\emph{(ii)}$\to$\emph{(iii)} is
proved from committed machinery: the two-fiber reduction
(Lemma~\ref{lem:twofiber}: the cascade step \emph{is} a
dilate-intersection of census solution sets), the drift law
(Lemma~\ref{lem:rungT}, machine-verified $3{,}528/3{,}528$), the
reflection identification (Lemma~\ref{lem:reflect}), the
$T$-free fiber machinery, and the lossless identity of
Corollary~\ref{cor:tau}. The arithmetical content---that the per-fiber
cut beats the pool within $C(T,k)$ fibers---is proved \emph{given} the
ledger's inputs, by the induction of Proposition~\ref{prop:Hq}. None of those inputs is silently established, and none
is silently weakened: that is the content of Table~\ref{tab:ledger}. The
$pq$ side of the composite frontier is carried by the row translation
(Theorem~\ref{thm:R}) with the same input structure; Theorem~\ref{thm:SC}
is stated for the $p^2$ side only. The reduction chain (i)$\to$(ii)$\to$(iii) is stated
over the $\pm$-representative fiber set $U_\pm$: (iii) is cell emptiness,
not LRC at the rung.

\paragraph{The free-parameter disclosure.}
Standing practice for this paper, applied to every constant entering
Theorem~\ref{thm:SC}: a claim is called \emph{derived} only when it is
derived outright, and a formula containing a fitted constant is labeled
as such. The constants are:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \emph{$c_1$ (H1g):} bounded by data only---$\le 0.214$ at $T=8$
  over the three census primes $p=17,19,29$, tightening with $k$
  (Table~\ref{tab:volume}); \emph{not proved}. The chain class, the only
  proved case, carries $c_1\approx 2.0$--$3.6$. The threshold the
  constant must beat is structural, not fitted: the trivial bound
  realizes $c_1=\bigl(T/(T-6)\bigr)^{\,m-1}$
  (Proposition~\ref{prop:threshold}), so the content of H1g is the
  margin over $\approx250$---which every measured constant carries and
  no proved constant yet does.
\item \emph{$\Lambda_0\approx 4$ (H2r):} data-chosen---the $\bar\lambda$ zone in
which the measured per-fiber cuts at the committed cells first exceed the random
model by $\sim 10\times$. In the $\pm$-reduced model the weak-cut fibers are
excluded from $U_\pm$ rather than added as a tail, so $\Lambda_0$ enters only
the definition of $U_\pm$, not an additive $N_{\mathrm{res}}$ term; it is
\emph{a data-chosen zone cutoff}, not a fitted depth constant.
\item \emph{$\gamma_0$ (H2m):} bounded by data ($\le 41$ in the
  non-resonant zone over the committed cells); proved only for the chain
  class, with loose constants.
\item \emph{$R$ (a junction-volume scale fitted at $4.2$ in an earlier
  formulation):} \emph{retired}. For the chain class it is replaced by
  the derived overlap budget $T-6$; for the thin families it is
  superseded by $c_1$ under H1g. No fitted $R$ remains in
  Theorem~\ref{thm:SC}.
\end{itemize}
The depth formula's remaining constants are structural: the leading
term is built from the overlap budget $T-6$ and the modulus $T$, and the
$\pm$-representative fiber set $U_\pm$ is defined by the reflection
identification (Lemma~\ref{lem:reflect}), with $\Lambda_0$ only the
data-chosen zone cutoff excluding the weak-cut fibers.

\paragraph{The sampled numbers, with intervals.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:SC}'s calibration rests on exhaustive censuses where
the cells are enumerable and on disclosed samples where they are not.
The $T=8$ saturation figures of Table~\ref{tab:pool}
($1.000/0.571/0.357/0.133/0.015$ at $p=17/19/23/29/31$) are exhaustive
censuses and carry no sampling error. The $T=9$ figures are
stride-sampled with full decisions per sampled tuple: at $p=29$,
saturation $0.903$, $95\%$ Wilson interval $[0.889,0.915]$
($n=2{,}005$), projected pool $1.39\times10^5$ with interval
$[1.37,1.41]\times10^5$; at $p=31$, saturation $0.490$,
$[0.468,0.511]$ ($n=2{,}002$), projected pool $1.18\times10^5$,
$[1.12,1.23]\times10^5$. Both pools are \emph{projected}---the
falling-factorial bound times the sampled fraction, at one prime
each---and the rung-monotone reading rests on two primes with
$\epsilon\in\{2,4\}$, whose modulation ($0.90$ against $0.49$) is large
and unmodeled. The $T=10$ saturation (extrapolated $\ge 0.9$ at the
$40\%$ budget from the rung-monotone pattern---two primes, unmodeled
$\epsilon$-modulation, no error control) is \emph{not measured}. Cascade-depth figures at the
committed cells are exact runs (median $2$, maximum $4$ over the zoo
random-$40$); the maxima $9/11/10$ are oriented-fiber counts (both $j$
and $-j$ processed) and exceed the $\pm$-reduced formula by the reflection
duplicate; the $\pm$-reduced maxima are $\le 6$ at $T=9$ and match the
formula of Table~\ref{tab:depthcal} to within the $O(1)$ error floor,
quoted as measurements.

\paragraph{Scope levels, fixed once.}
Three different closures circulate in this paper and must not be
conflated: \emph{cells} (the $T=7$ and $T=8$-critical cells are closed
within the tested primes, $p\le 61$, by exact census plus the shear
step); \emph{rungs} (the frontier cells of every $T\ge 8$ are closed
only conditionally, by Theorem~\ref{thm:SC} under its ledger); and
\emph{rigid-zone finiteness} (open; the five-prime zone of
\S\ref{sec:rigid} is an empirical finding of the verified range).
Statements below use the matching noun.

\paragraph{Where the derivation lives.}
Section~\ref{sec:scale} derives the cascade model and proves the proved
pieces (Lemmas~\ref{lem:twofiber}, \ref{lem:pool}, \ref{lem:spacing},
\ref{lem:reflect}, \ref{lem:renorm}; Theorem~\ref{thm:chaindilate});
reports the measurements with their scopes and ground-truth records;
states the two open obligations (the strand census for H1g, the sharp
pair-line constants for H2m) in their reduced forms; and closes with
the epistemic status. Theorem~\ref{thm:SC} should be read as the
summary of that section, not as an independent result.
